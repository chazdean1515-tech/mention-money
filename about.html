About

Mention Money is a daily research board for Kalshi Mentions markets. It is owned by Charlie O'Brien. The display name is Mention Money, and the X account is @MentionMoneyHQ.

The board lists events whose researched start time is today in Eastern Time. For each word it shows a short note, our probability estimate, the snapshot price, and the gap after a taker-fee estimate. When a featured card is still ahead of its event, it states our lean for that day.

Prices come from Kalshi's public API as a snapshot. They are not a live feed. The page says “Prices as of” and warns when the snapshot is old. Windows that have already started are left off the tape.

Estimates are opinions based on public transcripts, settled markets, and the notes published with each event. They can be wrong. This site does not place trades and does not have accounts.

The Kalshi buttons are a referral link. Mention Money earns a bonus if you sign up and trade through it. Optional SOL support is explained in the support dialog on the home page. See Terms and Privacy.
