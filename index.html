How to read this

Price = current Kalshi yes ask (cost to buy YES) / no ask shown as implied YES bid. Est. = my estimated probability the word is said. Edge = estimate minus the price you'd pay on the relevant side, net of taker fee ≈ 0.07·p·(1−p) per contract. Positive edge on YES means buy YES at the ask; on NO means buy NO at the no ask.

Estimates are judgment calls from public research, not guarantees. Read-only; no trading. Always check the market's rules text (hover/tap “rules”) — exact-phrase, plural and speaker requirements matter.
