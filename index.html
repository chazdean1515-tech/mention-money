          How the Mention Money Score works

          Score is 0–100, a percentile across every coin on the board. It mixes four things, compared within each chain: M, how many distinct X accounts posted the contract address or cashtag in 24 hours, weighted by followers (under 1K = 0.5, 1K–50K = 1, over 50K = 2, skipping new or bot-like accounts; estimated from a sample of up to 25 posts per coin and scaled to the full count); V, 24h volume; A, posts in the last full hour divided by the 24h hourly average (capped at 10); and L, liquidity up to $50K. Raw = (0.40·M + 0.35·V + 0.25·A) × L. When mention data is missing, the missing terms are dropped and the rest reweighted, and the score says "volume-only". A coin with a red flag (top 10 holders over 50%, mint or freeze not renounced, failed honeypot check) has its score halved.

          "Early or shilled": many mentions, little volume. "Whale-driven": big volume, few mentions. Safety data comes from RugCheck (Solana) and GoPlus (Base, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain). These checks miss things. We list coins with at least $50K liquidity in pools under a year old, and drop majors, stablecoins and tokenized assets.
